USDT to AED calculator

Convert between USDT and UAE dirham (AED) using a rate you enter. This page does not fetch live prices.

Default 3.6725: the AED is pegged at about 3.6725 per US dollar, and USDT usually trades near $1. Real quotes vary — enter the rate you are actually offered.

How it works

AED = USDT × rate. USDT = AED ÷ rate. Type in either box and the other updates. If a quote differs from 3.6725, the difference is the spread or fee you are paying.
