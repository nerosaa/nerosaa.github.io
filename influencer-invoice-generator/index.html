What to put on an influencer invoice

  • Each deliverable on its own line (e.g. 1 × Instagram Reel, 3 × Stories) so the brand can match it to the brief.
  • Usage rights and how long they last — paid-ad usage or whitelisting is usually priced separately.
  • Clear payment terms (Net 30 etc.), the currency, and exactly where to pay. For USDT, state the network (TRC20 / ERC20 / BEP20).
  • A unique, sequential invoice number — try the invoice number generator.

Not tax or legal advice. Check what your country requires on an invoice.
