Make a clean, sequential numbering series for your own invoices — e.g. INV-2026-001, INV-2026-002… Sequential only, so your records stay in order.

Invoice numbering tips

  • Use one continuous sequence and never reuse or skip a number — many tax offices expect this.
  • Adding the year (INV-2026-001) makes it easy to restart numbering each year if your local rules allow it.
  • Keep a log of which client each number went to.
