USDT ERC20 gas fee calculator

Estimate what a USDT transfer on Ethereum costs. You enter the numbers from your wallet — this page does not fetch live data.

~65,000 is typical for a USDT (ERC20) transfer. First-time sends to a new address can use more.

How the fee is calculated

Fee (ETH) = gas price (gwei) × gas limit ÷ 1,000,000,000. Fee (USD) = fee in ETH × ETH price. Your wallet usually shows the current gas price; the actual gas used is often a bit below the limit.

ERC20 vs TRC20 vs BEP20

For small payouts, ERC20 is usually the most expensive way to move USDT. TRC20 (Tron) and BEP20 (BNB Chain) are often much cheaper. See the USDT network fees comparison to pick a chain — and always confirm with the payer which network they will send on.
